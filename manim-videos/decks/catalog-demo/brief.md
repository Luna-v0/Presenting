## Narrative
You arrive not knowing what the catalog can do or what it refuses to do.
Each layout shows itself, doing the job it exists for.
You leave able to pick one per beat without reading the source.

## Why I'm presenting
This deck is the working sample. It exists so every layout gets rendered by a
real compile rather than only by a unit test, and so the catalog can be looked
at instead of read.

## What the audience should do
Recognise each layout on sight and know which beat shape it suits.

The identity itself, and how the pipeline stages fit together — those are
